Pocket Links

Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

Short version: there's no account and we don't collect your personal data. Your progress is saved on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device data to show them. You choose whether that data can be used to personalise ads.

Your progress

Your progress (coins, gems, clubs, your golfer, scores, career and settings) is saved on your iPhone. If iCloud is on, a backup copy is kept in your own iCloud account so you can carry on after reinstalling or on a new phone. Apple stores it under Apple's Privacy Policy. It isn't sent to us.

Settings › Reset all progress clears it from your iPhone and from the iCloud backup.

Ads

The free version of Pocket Links shows ads through Google AdMob: ads you choose to watch for a reward, and an ad after some rounds. Pocket Links Pro turns off the ads between rounds.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • device information, such as the model, iOS version and an advertising identifier
  • your IP address, which gives an approximate location
  • how you interact with ads
  • diagnostics, such as crashes and performance

Google handles this data under its own policies. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • Tracking. Before the first ad, iOS asks whether Pocket Links can track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, Google doesn't receive your advertising identifier. You can change this any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Consent. Where the law asks for it (for example in the EU and UK), Google's consent form appears before any ads, and you can change your answer in Settings › Privacy choices.
  • No personalised ads. If you don't allow tracking or consent, ads are still shown but aren't personalised.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, the app sends your scores and achievements to Apple so they can appear on leaderboards. Apple handles that data under Apple's Privacy Policy. We don't receive your Game Center details.

Purchases

In-app purchases (such as Pocket Links Pro, gem packs and the Season Pass) are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app only learns what you bought so it can add it to your game.

Daily reminder

If you turn on the daily reminder, the notification is scheduled on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to us, and you can turn it off in Settings or in iOS Settings › Notifications.

Friend challenges

A challenge code describes a round (course, wind and scores). It contains no personal information, and it's only shared if you choose to send it.

What we don't do

  • No account or sign-up.
  • No analytics of our own, and we never sell your data.
  • The app doesn't ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Children

Pocket Links is made for a general audience, not specifically for children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above before the change reaches the app.
