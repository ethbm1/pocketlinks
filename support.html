Common questions

How do I swing?
Drag down for power, then flick up. Up-left draws the ball, up-right fades it.
How do I change shot?
Tap Shot to step through the shots that club can play, such as Full, Punch, Chip and Flop.
My purchase didn't arrive.
Close and reopen the app; anything paid for is added on launch. For Pocket Links Pro, use Settings › Restore Purchases.
Will I lose my progress?
Progress is saved on your iPhone and kept through updates. With iCloud on, it's also backed up to your iCloud, so it comes back if you reinstall or move to a new iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account.
How do I turn off ads?
Pocket Links Pro removes the ads between rounds. Ads for rewards are always your choice.
How do I change my ad and tracking choices?
In the EU and UK, use Settings › Privacy choices. For tracking, go to iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
It runs slowly on my phone.
Turn on Battery saver in Settings for a lighter look and smoother play.
